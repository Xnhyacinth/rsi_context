## What this matrix supports

- The S₀→S₁ contrast holds across **three independent Option-2 world
  segments** (real documents, disjoint rows, entity types spanning
  works/professionals/sites) plus the authored surfaces — the world
  axis now has 3 real + 3 authored worlds behind the contrast.
- The author-variance floor gives the researcher arm's variance a
  number: at this endpoint, 1-of-3 draws succeed, so single-run
  researcher results are unrepresentative by construction — a
  0-vs-6/6 single-run difference would be within authoring variance.
  Any researcher-arm claim must report the draw distribution, not one
  draw.

## What it does not support

- Significance claims (unchanged). The world axis has 3 real segments
  per the binding-N guidance (tens needed for score-graded deltas);
  gate-binary contrasts are large effects at this size.
- The empty-reply failures are the DS endpoint's reliability, conflated
  with authoring variance in the floor number — an improver-side
  reliability gate (retry on empty) would separate transport failures
  from genuine strategy variance. Recorded as the known conflation,
  not hidden.
- The fixed arm's 0/6 on Option-2 slot cells with zephyr's first
  candidate (play-the-game, non-scope) remains the designed exception
  (wiring tests pin the semantics).
